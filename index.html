04 — FAQ

Straight
answers.

How long does a full store build take? +

Typically 4–6 weeks from kickoff to launch. Complex headless builds or custom apps can run 8–12 weeks. We fix the timeline before we start — and we hit it.

What does a maintenance retainer include? +

A monthly bank of dev/design hours, 24h SLA on critical bugs, performance monitoring, app updates, and a quarterly CRO roadmap. Unused hours roll over.

Can you run our Black Friday / product launch? +

Yes — it's one of our specialties. Campaign landing pages, countdowns, load testing, email/SMS flows and live support during the event. Check our and pages.

Do you work with stores outside Shopify? +

We migrate them in. WooCommerce, Magento, custom stacks — we've moved 40+ stores to Shopify with zero SEO loss and full data integrity.
